About Hooker's Spirit

Hooker's Spirit was founded in 2020 with a singular mission: to preserve, celebrate, and share the transformative legacy of John Lee Hooker, one of America's most influential blues musicians. Born from a deep appreciation for Hooker's revolutionary contributions to blues music and his profound impact on generations of artists, our organization emerged as a response to the need for a comprehensive, accessible platform dedicated to his life, work, and enduring cultural significance. What began as a passionate project by blues historians and musicians has evolved into a thriving community of over 1,000 dedicated members worldwide, all united by their love for authentic blues music and Hooker's pioneering spirit.

At the heart of Hooker's Spirit lies our unwavering commitment to authenticity, education, and community engagement. We believe that John Lee Hooker's music transcends generations and genres, carrying messages of resilience, creativity, and the transformative power of artistic expression. Our core values—honoring tradition while embracing innovation, making blues history accessible to everyone, and fostering a global community of musicians and enthusiasts—guide every initiative we undertake. Through meticulously curated masterclasses, digitally preserved archival materials, exclusive merchandise, and vibrant community events, we ensure that Hooker's legacy continues to inspire, educate, and move audiences for generations to come. We are more than a platform; we are custodians of a musical heritage that defines American culture.
